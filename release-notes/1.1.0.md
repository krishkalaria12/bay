## Added

- **New spaces, step by step.** The + at the sidebar's foot walks you through a space: its name and icon, its look,
  and whether it shares sign-ins with another space.
- **A look for each space.** Bay starts on the Twilight theme, and every new space gets a look of its own.
- **The space's title in the sidebar**, with its menu, renaming, and a + for a new tab, folder, space or Split View.
- **Clear inactive tabs** from the sidebar, their rows breaking into pixels as they go.
- **Settings, rebuilt** as a sidebar of panes, with search.
- **Every emoji** to pick an icon from, by category.
- **Updates in the sidebar.** A waiting update shows as a row over the sidebar's foot.

## Improved

- **New tabs at the top.** New installs add new tabs under the New Tab row, and the row stays first even when the
  list is long. Settings › Tabs › Add new tabs to the top switches it.
- **Empty folders** say to drag tabs in, and have their own menu.
- **A wider sidebar** by default, so its toggle shows.
- **Light or dark stays yours** when a page's colour tints the window.

## Fixed

- **Spaces keep their browsing data apart**, including spaces that shared a profile in 1.0.
- **A shared profile syncs once**, and stays when one of its spaces is deleted.
- **Reopening a closed window** shows the tab it was showing.
- **Favorites and the address field** stay solid when the window is solid.
- **Onboarding** shows your installed browsers' icons, and previews a space's gradient theme.
