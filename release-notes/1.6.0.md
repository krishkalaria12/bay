headline: Bay 1.6: Tidy Tabs, a built-in content blocker and a faster browser
---

## Added

- **Tidy Tabs.** Once six tabs are open, Tidy appears in the sidebar and sorts your loose tabs into named groups, on
  your Mac. ⌘Z puts them back. Turn it on or off in Settings › Tabs.
- **Undo and redo in the search bar.**

## Improved

- **Content blocking.** Bay now blocks ads and trackers with its own built-in blocker instead of uBlock Origin, and
  uses the same kind of filter lists. It also strips tracking parameters from links and refuses popups and pages the
  lists block.
- **Tidy Downloads** renames files with machine names as they finish downloading, instead of asking each time. You can
  undo each rename, and files you named yourself are left alone.
- **Protected media.** Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and other streaming sites open in Safari's engine, which has the
  DRM they need. Bay also moves any other page there when its protected video won't play, and tells you when it does.
  Settings › Protected Media and Site Controls explain what's happening.
- **Library** opens in the sidebar, with an option to open it as a page. Spaces are laid out in columns, and the
  library's sidebar follows yours to the right.
- **Space themes** can be edited from the sidebar menu, and from the command bar while tabs are open.
- **Tab groups** drop their background and indent their tabs.
- **The tab menu** is shorter, with rarer actions under More.
- **Speed.** The sidebar, the command bar, saving your session and background page updates all do less work, and
  background tabs sleep sooner on Macs with less memory.

## Fixed

- **The search bar** switches to an open tab when you type its site's address, and ranks pages you've visited above
  search suggestions.

## Removed

- **The engine switch.** Bay now chooses the engine for each page, so the choices in Settings › Engines and Site
  Controls' "Opens in" are gone.
