## Added

- **Capture.** Press `⇧⌘2` to capture part of a page, then copy, save, share or mark it up. Capture Full Page
  saves the whole page, and Capture in Portrait frames it on your space's colours.
- **Easels and Library.** Collect captures, images, links, text and drawings on a whiteboard. Move things around,
  make captures live, or export an Easel as a PNG or HTML file. Library keeps your saved media, downloads,
  Easels and archived tabs together.
- **Boosts.** Change a site's colours and fonts, remove parts of a page, focus on one section, or add CSS and
  JavaScript. Boosts sync between your devices; scripts stay on the Mac where you enabled them.
- **Sharing.** Share folders, split views and selected tabs, or turn selected text into a quote card.
- **Tidy Tabs.** Group today's tabs by topic from the sidebar menu or command bar. Pinned tabs can suggest
  shorter titles, and Tidy Downloads offers readable names for new downloads.
- **Link previews.** Hold Shift while hovering a link for five seconds to show a preview of its page.
- **Streaming sites in WebKit.** Supported streaming sites open in macOS's WebKit engine by default. Choose an
  engine for individual sites in Site Controls, or change the defaults in Settings › Engines.
- **Foldable JSON.** Developer Mode shows JSON responses as a tree, with the raw text a click away.

## Improved

- **Sidebar controls.** Press `⌘.` to focus the sidebar and navigate with the arrow keys. `⌘Z` undoes sidebar
  edits, holding Option reveals a hidden sidebar, and mouse side buttons switch spaces while over it.
- **Files in the sidebar.** Drop Finder files and folders into pinned tabs. Expand folders in place, open files
  in tabs, or drag them onto a website to upload them.
- **Little Bay.** Open it from any app with a shortcut or the menu bar. Idle windows move into Library's archive
  after six hours.
- **Search and meetings.** `⇧Return` opens a search's top result, and `⌃⌘J` joins the meeting that's on now.
- **Favorites and long lists.** Favorites show unread counts from page titles, and long lists use a new renderer.

## Fixed

- **Space switching.** Reorder spaces while swiping and switch them from the sidebar's peek.
- **Window controls.** The window buttons appear with the closed sidebar's peek.
- **External links.** Links from other apps open in the most recent space by default.
- **Tidy Downloads.** Renamed files keep their names after relaunching, and older downloads aren't offered again.
