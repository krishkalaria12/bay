headline: Bay 1.7: a new icon you can choose, better Zen import and steadier tabs
---

## Added

- **A new icon.** Bay's icon is now a half sun setting over the bay. Pick another in Settings › Appearance: Navy,
  Coral, Noir, Cobalt, or the Golden Gate icon from earlier versions. The Dock shows your pick even while Bay is closed.

## Improved

- **Space icons.** When you have more spaces than the sidebar has room for, the current space and the spaces on either
  side keep their icons, and the rest become dots.
- **Importing from Zen** brings over your spaces, Essentials, pinned tabs that weren't loaded yet, and logins
  protected by a primary password.
- **Sleeping tabs.** macOS can report memory pressure for hours while plenty is free. Bay no longer puts background
  tabs to sleep at once when that happens. It puts them to sleep sooner instead.

## Fixed

- **Window size.** New browser windows open at the size of your last window.
- **Arc import** keeps the spaces you chose.
- **Accounts you disconnect** no longer come back by themselves.
- **Calendar.** When a refresh fails, your last events stay on screen.
- **Screen sharing** uses the latest request when a site asks more than once.
