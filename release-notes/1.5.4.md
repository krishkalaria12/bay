## Added

- **The bay command.** Script Bay from the terminal: open tabs, read pages and history, and install user scripts that
  run on the sites they match. Install it from Settings › Advanced › Terminal, and turn on "Let the bay command
  control Bay" to allow it. Its tabs open behind the page you're on, and private windows never load its scripts.
- **Pinned split views.** Pin a split view to the sidebar and it reopens side by side.
- **Reorder spaces** by dragging their icons at the bottom of the sidebar.
- **Clear Cookies and Cache** from the command bar.
- **Update channels.** Choose Stable or Nightly in Settings › General. Nightly gets new builds first.

## Improved

- **Setup.** Drag your old browser onto Bay to import from it. You can skip steps, pick up where you left off, and
  search during setup.
- **Link previews** spring out of the link you clicked and back into it when closed.
- **One space** hides the sidebar's space header.
- **Sidebar on the right.** The window buttons sit in its header, as they do on the left, instead of on a band over
  the page.
- **The installer** opens in a styled window.

## Fixed

- **Pages without a background of their own** show white, or their dark-mode canvas, instead of the window's colours
  under their text.
