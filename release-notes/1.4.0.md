## Added

- **Speed and subtitles on the floating video.** Picture in Picture has a speed menu (0.5× to 2×) and a subtitles
  menu, and shows the subtitles you pick, YouTube's included. Your choice carries back to the page.
- **Live streams say Live.** The floating video and the sidebar player show Live, or how far behind you are
  ("5m behind"). Click it to jump back to live.
- **Search shortcuts.** Type a site's shortcut and press Tab to search it (`yt` → Tab → funny cats), or switch to
  DuckDuckGo-style bangs (`!yt funny cats`) in Settings › Search. Common shortcuts are ready to customize.
- **Emoji, units and currency in the command bar.** Type an emoji's name, `5 km in miles` or `100 usd in inr`, and
  Return copies the answer.
- **Cookie banners blocked** by default, with the full uBlock Origin.

## Improved

- **Link rules work like Arc's Air Traffic Control.** Match links that contain or equal some text, send them to a
  space, your most recent space, Little Bay or a new window, and choose where every other link goes. Peek's Open as
  Tab follows your rules too.
- **The address bar lists matching sites** ahead of search suggestions, and a site search takes the site's colour.
- **Saved passwords and cards are better guarded.** Touch ID always asks before they show or are exported.
- **Bay sits still.** It idles under half a percent of CPU with a tab open.
- **Folding a space animates**, and top tabs shrink to their icons before the strip scrolls.
- **The command bar** stays open across space switches, and its arrow keys wrap around.
- **History** stays quick with thousands of pages.

## Fixed

- **The sidebar's update button** works while an update downloads in the background.
- **Clicking a tab** works while the command bar is open.
- **The default browser action** no longer shows when Bay is already your default browser.
- **A new profile** no longer creates a Chromium Apps folder in `~/Applications`.
