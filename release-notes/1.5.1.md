## Fixed

- **Full-screen video on streaming sites** fills the screen without leaving the Bay window behind on your desktop,
  as on every other site. The window comes back when you leave full screen, and switching tabs while a video is full
  screen takes it out of full screen.
- **Streaming sites** no longer cover the tab you're looking at after you switch spaces.
- **Window controls** that appear when you hover over the window's corner have a background, so they stay readable
  over the page.
