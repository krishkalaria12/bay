## Added

- **Folders inside folders.** Nest pinned folders up to five levels deep, move them with drag or Move to Folder, and
  undo a move with ⌘Z. They sync too, and an import from Arc keeps its folder tree.
- **Rules for links from other apps.** In Settings › Links, send a link to a space, Little Bay or a new window by its
  site (`github.com/acme/*`, `*.example.com`) and, if you like, the app it came from. Rules sync with your settings.
- **Every playing tab in one place.** The sidebar's player lists all the tabs making sound, with Pause All and Go to
  Tab.
- **Keep sites awake.** Choose sites whose tabs never sleep, in Settings › Tabs or from a tab's menu.
- **Compact density.** Smaller sidebar rows, pinned tiles and address field, in Settings › Appearance.
- **Fold a space.** Click its name to hide its pinned tabs and folders and keep today's tabs in view.
- **Search in Settings.** Find a setting by name and jump straight to it.
- **Extensions in private windows.** Allow an extension there from Settings › Extensions.
- **Copy File and Copy Path** for downloads.
- **Choose where Bay starts:** a new tab, or the last tab you viewed.

## Improved

- **Links from other apps open in Bay's window** by default instead of Little Bay (Settings › Links changes it).
- **A closed pinned tab** shows an × that removes it.
- **With the sidebar closed,** the address bar stays in its peek, and the peek hides when the pointer leaves.
- **The extensions menu** fits five per row.

## Fixed

- **Sites stay signed in** across a quit: session cookies are no longer lost while windows close.
- **Now Playing** shows only media you've heard, not muted autoplay or short sound effects.
