## Fixed

- **Full-screen video on streaming sites** keeps playing on its own desktop when you swipe to another one. It used to
  leave full screen and bring the Bay window back.

## Removed

- **Battery Saver.** Bay no longer pauses busy background tabs on battery or in Low Power Mode, and the setting is
  gone from Settings › Advanced.
