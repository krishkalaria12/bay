## Added

- **Extensions in the import.** Bring the extensions from Chrome, Arc, Helium and other Chromium browsers, installed
  into the space you import into, with their settings. Firefox add-ons come along when everything they use works the
  same in Chrome (without their settings).
- **An emoji for every space.** Each new space gets one of its own, and you can pick another.
- **Arrow through suggestions** in the command bar, and the field fills with the one you're on.

## Improved

- **Imported sign-ins stay whole.** A site's cookies replace the space's own instead of mixing two sessions, so you
  arrive signed in.
- **Pull request previews** read GitHub's search, so they show up again.
- **The sidebar's New Tab row** has a softer hover.

## Fixed

- **Google and Gmail stay signed in** after an import from Chrome, Helium or another browser that ties its Google
  sign-in to your Mac.
- **File pickers** let you choose every type a site accepts, not just the first.
- **Switching tabs** gives the page keyboard focus.
- **A fullscreen video in Picture in Picture** stays there when you leave its space.
- **Renaming in the sidebar** no longer closes as soon as it opens.
- **The sidebar follows space switches** everywhere.
