Bay mobile: what to build next

Motion is the biggest gap you can feel today. The biggest gap that breaks sites is pages that ask a question: alerts, permissions, file pickers and links to other apps. This plan compares Bay with Arc Search and Lens, lists what is still missing, and splits the work into small PRs.

Builds on docs/mobile-plan.html (the roadmap) and docs/mobile-ios-parity-plan.html (the 5 November gap audit). Item ids (I, A, B) are the ones used there. Statuses come from reading the code, not from running the apps.

Updated 10 November 2026: the animations (A1 to A8) landed in this PR, and the release setup (B8) with them. Everything else the plan lists was already on main; docs/mobile-ios-parity-plan.html keeps the record of what landed and what is still open.

01 · Animations

Motion: A1 to A8

Bay animates the bar hiding on scroll, the bar swipe between tabs, the stacked switcher, toasts and download rows. All of it uses Reanimated. Sheets open with the library's default spring, there are no shared transitions between screens, and haptics exist only on the iOS pull to refresh. Arc and Lens feel fast because each tap gets a response within one frame.

Transform and opacityNever animate width, height or layout props on the page path.
UI threadReanimated worklets only, nothing driven from JS state.
150 to 250 msSprings for drags, timing curves for taps.
Reduced motionuseReducedMotion() turns every one into a crossfade.

03 · Missing on both apps

Fix sites first, then add features

One engine event, onPageRequest, plus one shared sheet covers four of these: dialogs, HTTP sign-in, permissions and links to other apps. That keeps the no-Material rule on Android. None of the native hooks exist yet.

06 · Not building

Left out on purpose

Browse for Me, Pinch to Summarize and Call Arc: Arc is turning them off, Lens has none, and they need a paid server.

Blur or imitation glass on Android: Android stays flat, as decided on 2 November.

Spaces, sync and tablet layouts: after the first release, as in the roadmap.
