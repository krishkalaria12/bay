What Arc has that Bay doesn't

Every feature Arc for macOS shipped from 2021 to its sunset notice, checked against Bay's code. The plan lists what Bay already matches, the gaps left, the things to re-test, and the order to build them in.

Summary

Bay already matches most of Arc: spaces with themes and profiles, favorites, pinned tabs and nested folders, auto cleanup, Peek, Split View, Little Bay, link rules (Arc's Air Traffic Control), the command bar with actions and site search, Capture, Easels, the Library, previews, live folders and calendars, and the media players. The review found 22 gaps and 7 things to re-test.

The gaps are of four kinds:

  • Small habits Arc users will miss on day one. Unread counts on favorites, opening Little Bay from any app, mouse buttons that switch spaces, Shift-Return for the top result. Ten items, mostly S.
  • Keyboard and undo. Moving around the sidebar from the keyboard, ⌘Z for any sidebar edit, and files from Finder in the sidebar.
  • Boosts. Arc's way to restyle or zap parts of a site. Bay has no answer except uBlock's cosmetic filters. One large build.
  • Sharing and Arc Max. Shared folders, Share Quote, Easel links, and the "tidy" AI features. Each needs a decision before work starts.

Phase 1 is sized to land before 22 December 2026, when Arc stops opening. The later phases don't depend on that date.

Arc's sunset

The Browser Company announced on 22 October 2026 that Arc will be sunset. New accounts stopped that day. On 23 November Arc starts showing sunset reminders. On 22 December Arc for macOS and Windows stops working, sign-in ends and all Arc services are turned off. The official path is to move to Dia.

That gives Bay 58 days during which every Arc user is choosing a browser. Bay's Arc importer reads StorableSidebar.json straight from disk (packages/import/ios/Core/Discovery.swift), so it should keep working after Arc stops opening, as long as the files stay on disk. Re-test it against a profile from Arc's final build (see Re-test).

Arc removed some features itself before the end. They are not gaps:

  • Ask on Page, removed from Arc Max on 28 August 2025.
  • Arc Notes, phased out in April 2024. Bay's command bar already opens new notes in Notion, Confluence, Google Docs and Word.
  • Reader Mode, turned off on 6 June 2024.
  • GitHub previews, replaced by GitHub Live Folders in May 2024. Bay has both.
  • The Recent Tab indicator (removed February 2024) and the iCloud sidebar sync (replaced by Arc Sync, June 2024).

Phase 1

Before Arc shuts down

10 × S

Small habits Arc users will reach for in their first week. Each one ships alone. Land them before Arc's reminders start on 23 November if possible, and before 22 December at the latest.

1 · Unread counts on favorites S

Arc badged Slack, X, Google Calendar and Gmail favorites with their counts. Read the count from the page title ("Inbox (3)", "(2) Slack") and from favicon badges, and draw it on the Essentials tile. Keep a short list of title patterns per site, and fall back to a dot when a site only changes its favicon.

Where
sidebar/PinnedGrid.tsx, media/TabBadges.tsx, lib/siteIcons.ts
  • A Gmail favorite shows the inbox count and clears when it reaches zero, also while asleep after its last count.

2 · Little Bay from any app S

Arc had a menu bar button for Little Arc. Add an optional menu bar item and a global shortcut (default ⌥⌘N, settable, off until chosen) that open a Little Bay window with the command bar focused, even when Bay is in the background.

Where
packages/shell/ios/AppModule.swift (status item, global hot key), littleBay/actions.ts, Settings › Links
  • From Finder, the shortcut opens Little Bay ready to type. Clicking the menu bar item does the same. Turning it off unregisters the key.

3 · Little Bay windows put away after six hours S

Arc closed idle Little Arc windows after six hours and kept their links in the archive, filtered as Little Arc. Do the same with a setting for the time, and add "Show/Hide All Little Bay Windows" to the Dock menu.

Where
littleBay/, lib/tabLifecycle.ts, packages/shell/ios/DockSelection.swift, Library archive filter
  • A Little Bay left alone for six hours closes, and its page is in the Library's archive under Little Bay.

4 · Mouse buttons 4 and 5 switch spaces S

Arc let mice without a trackpad cycle spaces with the side buttons. Bay handles only the middle button (AppKit's buttonNumber 2). Map the side buttons (buttonNumber 3 and 4) to Previous and Next Space when the pointer is over the sidebar, and keep Back and Forward over the page.

Where
packages/shell/ios/System.swift (otherMouseUp), layout/spacePager.ts
  • Over the sidebar the side buttons switch spaces with the swipe animation; over a page they go back and forward.

5 · Option shows the hidden sidebar S

With the sidebar auto-hidden, holding Option slides it in, and releasing slides it out. A setting turns it off. The shell already watches modifier changes for ⌃Tab, so this adds Option to that monitor.

Where
packages/shell/ios/ShellModule.swift (flagsChanged), layout/useSidebarDock.ts
  • Holding Option alone shows the sidebar; Option as part of a shortcut or while typing doesn't.

6 · Current tab out of view S

When the selected tab is scrolled out of the sidebar, show a small marker at the top or bottom edge. Clicking it scrolls to the tab.

Where
components/Sidebar.tsx, sidebar/geometry.ts
  • With forty tabs, the marker shows at the edge nearest the tab and disappears once the tab is in view, in both densities.

7 · Shift-Return opens the top result S

Arc's Instant Links skipped the results page. Shift-Return in the command bar sends the query through DuckDuckGo's "\" redirect (first result) using Bay's bang support, with no AI and no new service. The hint shows in the Return keycap.

Where
omnibox/open.ts, omnibox/bangs.ts, omnibox/Keycaps.tsx
  • "bay browser github" with Shift-Return lands on the repository, not a results page. Private windows behave the same.

8 · ⌃⌘J joins the meeting on now S

Add Join Meeting to the menus with ⌃⌘J, using the same path as the alert's Join button. It is disabled when no meeting is on.

Where
packages/shell/ios/Menus.swift, live/MeetingAlert.tsx
  • Two minutes before a Meet event, ⌃⌘J opens the call in the space set for it.

9 · Portrait capture S

Arc's Portrait Mode made a neat screenshot of the page in a phone-shaped frame with the theme behind it. Add "Capture in Portrait" to the command bar and Option-click on Capture Full Page.

Where
components/capture/, developer/screenshot.ts
  • The image is saved to Downloads and the Library, at 2× scale, framed in the space's colours.

10 · JSON viewer in Developer Mode S

Arc installed the JSON Formatter extension when Developer Mode was turned on. Bay can do better with no extension: format application/json responses on dev tabs with folding, a raw toggle and copy path.

Where
components/developer/, engine page script
  • A localhost JSON endpoint shows formatted with folding; Raw shows the original bytes; non-dev tabs are unchanged.

Exit criteria

  • All ten shipped in a release before 22 December, each with a native or unit check where the harness reaches it.
Phase 2

Keyboard & undo

2 × M1 × M

Arc's sidebar could be driven without a mouse, and almost every edit could be undone.

11 · Move around the sidebar from the keyboard M

⌘. focuses the sidebar. Arrow keys move between rows and into folders, Return opens, Space peeks, ⌫ closes, ⌃⌘R renames, Escape returns to the page. The focused row has a visible ring separate from the selection, and VoiceOver reads it.

Where
components/Sidebar.tsx, sidebar/state.ts, sidebar/entries.ts
  • Every row type (favorite, pinned, folder, group, live folder, split) can be reached and opened from the keyboard alone.

12 · ⌘Z for any sidebar edit M

Generalise the folder-move undo into one stack per window for sidebar edits: close, clear, move, pin, unpin, rename, icon, theme. ⌘Z applies when focus isn't in a text field or page editor, the same rule the folder undo already follows.

Where
lib/folderUndo.ts to a general stack, store/organize.ts, sidebar/clear.ts
  • Clear, then ⌘Z, brings every cleared tab back in place. Undo never fires while typing in a page.

13 · Files and folders from Finder M

Dragging a file or folder from Finder into the pinned section keeps it there. A file opens in a tab (PDFs, images, HTML) or with its app. A folder expands in place to show its files. Rows can be dragged into a page to upload. Synced as a bookmark-style path, shown as missing on a Mac without the file.

Where
store/types.ts, sidebar/dnd.tsx, sync schema, security-scoped bookmarks in the shell
  • A dropped PDF opens from the sidebar after relaunch. Dragging it into Gmail attaches it. A second Mac shows the row as unavailable.

Exit criteria

  • Keyboard pass with VoiceOver on; undo covers every listed edit; file rows survive relaunch and sync.
Phase 3

Boosts

LM

Change how a site looks without writing code, and with code if you want. Arc users who relied on Boosts will have no other way to keep them.

14 · Zap, colours, fonts and custom CSS L

A Boost belongs to a host and lives in Site Controls. The editor offers: Zap (click elements to hide them, stored as selectors), a colour tint, a font, page zoom, and a CSS field. Bay's theme colours are exposed as CSS variables, as Arc did, so a Boost can match the sidebar. Each Boost toggles on and off per site; a global switch turns all off. CSS Boosts sync.

Where
New lib/boosts.ts, site/SiteControls.tsx, engine content-script injection at document start, sync/
Reuses
The Capture element picker for Zap
  • Zapping an element on a news site hides it on reload and on a second Mac. Turning the Boost off restores the page without reload.

15 · JavaScript Boosts, kept on one Mac M

A JS field that runs in the page's isolated world. Arc stopped syncing JS Boosts in September 2024 after a security report and made users re-enable each one per device. Bay starts there: JS Boosts never sync, are off by default, and show a warning when first turned on.

Where
Same as 14, isolated world script, excluded from the sync schema
  • A JS Boost runs only on its host, only on the Mac where it was turned on, and never in private windows unless allowed.

Exit criteria

  • Boosts are created, toggled, synced (CSS only) and removed; a security review of injection is complete.
Phase 4

Sharing

Decision first

Arc shared things through its own servers, which are being turned off. Bay has none. Decide how to host before starting items 17 and 18.

16 · Share Quote M

Bay's selection popover already copies a link to the highlighted text. Add "Share Quote": render the quote, page title and site in the space's colours as an image, put the image and the text-fragment link on the pasteboard, and offer the share sheet. Done in the shell, needing no server.

Where
site/SelectionPopover.tsx, site/selection.ts, shell image rendering
  • Pasting into Messages gives the card image; pasting into a text field gives the link, which opens scrolled to the quote.

17 · Share a folder, split or selection as a link M

Recommended without a server: compress the titles and links into the fragment of a link on the Bay website (bay.krishkalaria.in/share#…). The fragment never reaches the server. The page lists the links in any browser and has "Add to Bay", which opens a bay:// link that imports it as a folder.

Where
sidebar/menus.ts, apps/web share page, URL scheme handler in AppDelegate.swift
  • A 30-tab folder shared to Chrome lists every link; "Add to Bay" recreates it with nested folders.

18 · Share an Easel as a web page L

Easels hold images, so they don't fit in a link. This needs storage somewhere: a hosted bucket with unlisted links, or the user's own iCloud Drive public link. Until decided, the HTML export stays the way to share.

Where
library/export.ts, plus whatever host is chosen

Exit criteria

  • Share Quote shipped; collection links shipped if the fragment approach is chosen; Easel hosting either decided or recorded as out of scope.
Phase 5

Arc Max, on device

Research gate

Arc Max's lasting features were the small "tidy" ones. Build them on Apple's on-device model so nothing leaves the Mac, if the research spike shows the quality is good enough.

Research spike first (S): run 200 real tab titles, 100 download names and 20 sets of today's tabs through the on-device model. Go ahead only if the results are better than the originals most of the time and fast enough to feel instant.

19 · Tidy Tab Titles M

When a tab is pinned, suggest a short name ("Inbox – Gmail" becomes "Gmail"). The original stays one click away as Reset Name, which Bay already has.

20 · Tidy Downloads S

Offer a readable name for downloads like IMG_20261024_8812.pdf, based on the page and file. Applied only when accepted, never to files the user named.

21 · Tidy Tabs M

A button over today's tabs groups them by topic into Bay's tab groups, with ⌘Z to undo (needs item 12). Pinned tabs and existing groups are left alone.

22 · 5-Second Previews M

Shift-hover a link to load it in the background and show a short summary card with Open, Peek and Copy. Uses the Peek loader and the on-device model.

Where
New shell module for Foundation Models; sidebar/, DownloadsPanel.tsx, layout/usePeek.ts
Availability
macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence on. Hidden elsewhere.

Exit criteria

  • Spike passed and recorded; each feature off by default, reversible, and invisible on Macs without the model.

Risks

Time to the sunset

58 days to 22 December. If Phase 1 slips, ship the items that are done; the import and the features already matched matter more than any single gap.

Boost injection

Boosts run in every matching page. Arc had a security problem here in September 2024. Keep JS local and opt-in, inject in an isolated world, and review before release.

Global shortcut clashes

⌥⌘N is used by other apps. The global shortcut must be settable, start off, and say when the key is taken.

Unread counts are fragile

Sites change their title formats. Keep the patterns in one place with tests, and fall back to a dot instead of a wrong number.

On-device model reach

Bay supports macOS 14, but the model needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence. Phase 5 reaches only part of the users; a cloud model would break Bay's privacy stance.

Links in fragments

Very large folders make long links, and anyone holding the link can read it. Cap the size and say so in the share sheet.

Assumptions in this plan

  • Arc's feature list comes from its macOS release notes (2021 to October 2026) and coverage of Arc Max. The 2025–2026 notes contain only fixes.
  • "Missing" is based on searching the code; the app was not run for this review.
  • Sizes are rough and assume one person familiar with the codebase.

Not in this plan

  • Features Arc removed: Ask on Page, Arc Notes, Reader Mode, People Cards, the Recent Tab indicator.
  • Arc account extras: membership cards, Gift Arc and referral-locked icons.
  • Phone apps and sync to iPhone: Arc Search is a separate product. Bay syncs between Macs and accepts Handoff.
  • Windows and Linux: covered by their own ports.
  • Previews for Notion, Figma, Linear, Outlook and Confluence, and search inside folder previews: deferred until asked for.
  • Earlier plan items that Arc never had: clipboard history, one-time codes, "Allow ads here", text from images and push notifications with the tab closed. They stay open but aren't Arc gaps.
